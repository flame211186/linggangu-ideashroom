# Commercial and hosted use

This repository is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0. It is not
licensed under an OSI-approved open-source license.

## Allowed

- personal use and modification;
- internal use by a company or other organization;
- redistribution of source or desktop builds when all ELv2 notices and
  conditions are preserved;
- building a separate product that uses limited LingGanGu functionality,
  provided users are not given access to a substantial set of LingGanGu
  features as a hosted or managed service.

## Not allowed without separate written permission

- offering LingGanGu, a modified LingGanGu, or a substantial set of its
  functionality to third parties as a hosted or managed service;
- operating a public or private multi-user LingGanGu instance for customers;
- removing license, copyright, modification, or trademark notices;
- representing a modified build as an official LingGanGu release.

The hosted-service restriction applies whether the service is free or paid.
Questions about a concrete use case require written permission from Huo_miao.
